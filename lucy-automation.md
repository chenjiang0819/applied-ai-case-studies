# Lucy Automation

Built and operated.

## Problem

Infrastructure incidents across distributed operations required timely analysis, acknowledgement and troubleshooting guidance.

## My Role

I built and operate Lucy Automation, combining software implementation with ongoing technical delivery.

## What I Built

A 24/7 AI-enabled Zabbix integration using Python, HTTP and APIs to analyse operational events, automate event acknowledgement and generate troubleshooting guidance.

## Engineering Decisions

Integrated AI assistance directly into monitoring and incident-response workflows, connecting event analysis with actions and guidance for operational support.

## Outcome

Lucy Automation operates across more than 69 countries, reducing incident diagnosis and first-response time from approximately 12 hours to approximately 10 minutes.
