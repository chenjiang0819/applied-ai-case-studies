# Lucy Automation

Status: **Built and operated**.

## Problem
Recurring infrastructure incidents required diagnosis and initial response across geographically distributed operations.

## My Role
I built and operate Lucy Automation, combining hands-on software engineering with technical delivery.

## What I Built
AI-enabled monitoring and automation workflows. The public profile describes a 24/7 Zabbix integration using Python, HTTP and APIs for operational-event analysis, acknowledgement and troubleshooting guidance.

## Technical Decisions
Integrated AI assistance into monitoring workflows while maintaining operational ownership and support.

## Outcome
The public profile reports coverage across more than 69 countries and a reduction in incident diagnosis and first-response time from approximately 12 hours to approximately 10 minutes. These are publicly reported operational figures; this repository does not supply an independent benchmark or imply that every incident has the same outcome.

## Constraints and Responsible Deployment
Operational automation needs accountable ownership and careful handling of event information. Internal prompts, data, endpoints and configurations are omitted.
