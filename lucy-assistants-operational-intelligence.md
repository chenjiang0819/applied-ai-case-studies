# AI Assistants and Operational Intelligence

Built; recurring operational reporting.

## Problem

Smart-facility, green-energy and infrastructure work needed accessible project information and recurring diagnostic support.

## My Role

I designed and built assistant software and expanded infrastructure AI into operational intelligence.

## What I Built

An LLM- and RAG-based assistant with React and Node.js for interactive access to project and operational information. Automated pipelines using Cisco Meraki data and SFNOC workflows generate daily LLM-based security reports and AI-assisted diagnostics.

## Engineering Decisions

Combined retrieval with an interactive application for information access, and automated recurring reporting through operational data pipelines.

## Outcome

Delivered the assistant and recurring reporting/diagnostic workflows for smart-facility, green-energy and infrastructure support.
